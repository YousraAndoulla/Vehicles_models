Smart Vehicle Detection & Traffic Analysis
Upload images, videos, or connect a live stream—to detect vehicle types and estimate congestion.
Images

Upload a traffic image. Our system detects vehicles, classifies their types, analyzes flow and road occupancy, and estimates the congestion level.

Videos

Upload a traffic video. We use YOLO11x for vehicle type detection & tracking and YOLO11l-seg for road/lane/crosswalk segmentation, then aggregate flow and occupancy over time to estimate the congestion level.

Real Time

Connect a live camera or stream URL. In real time we run YOLO11x (vehicle type detection & tracking) and YOLO11l-seg (road/lane/crosswalk segmentation) to compute occupancy, volume-to-capacity, and the live congestion level.
